# LOKIVA — Live Hackathon Presentation Script & Demo Flow

Follow this exact 3-minute winning demo sequence during the hackathon pitch:

---

## 🎯 Step 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Explain the traveler friction: *"Traditional apps give endless lists of places, but don't know who you're with, how much money or time you have, or how hard it is to walk."*

---

## 🤖 Step 2: Natural Language AI Intent Extraction (0:30 - 1:15)
1. In the AI prompt bar (or on `/ai-guide`), enter:
   > **"I'm with my parents. We have 4 hours, ₹2,000, want local food and culture, and don't want much walking."**
2. Click **"Explore AI"**.
3. Point out:
   - **Extracted Constraints**: JSON Inspector extracts 240 mins, ₹2,000, family (4 pax), low walking.
   - **Explainability**: Every card displays **"Why this fits you"** verified bullets (e.g. `✓ Fits within ₹2,000 budget`, `✓ Seated ground-floor access`, `✓ 12 min from hotel`).

---

## 🗺️ Step 3: Feasibility Engine & Itinerary Timeline (1:15 - 2:00)
1. Click **"Build Itinerary From These"** (or navigate to `/itinerary`).
2. Show the **Feasibility Confidence Gauge** (92% - Excellent Fit).
3. Point out the sequenced timeline:
   - Start from Hotel
   - Auto-rickshaw transit durations (e.g. `🛺 15 min travel`)
   - 10 min rest/prep buffers
   - Budget & time balance meters

---

## 🌧️ Step 4: The Game Changer — Dynamic Re-Planning (2:00 - 2:30)
1. Click the **"Dynamic Re-Plan"** button.
2. Select **"Simulate Rain Alert"** and click **"Execute Re-Plan"**.
3. Watch LOKIVA detect the outdoor stop and replace it with a covered indoor cultural masterclass nearby while preserving the rest of the schedule, budget, and transit integrity!

---

## 🏢 Step 5: The Two-Sided Marketplace — Provider Hub (2:30 - 3:00)
1. Use the top navbar switcher to switch to **"Provider: Jaipur Artisan Guild"**.
2. Show that local artisans can see:
   - Views (1,420), Saves (385), Bookings (142), and Revenue (₹92,400)
   - Audience breakdown (Family 48%, Couples 26%)
   - 7-Day Demand trends
3. End with the tag line: **"LOKIVA: Find the place. Feel the local."**
